Privacy Policy — Fanous

Last updated: 21 November 2026 • Publisher: SwissyWorks • Contact: contact@swissyworks.com

In short: Fanous does not collect or transmit personal data. No ads, no trackers, no third-party analytics.

  • Stored on your device only: optional display name, chosen avatar letter, onboarding answers, game results and levels, reminder time.
  • Focus question: used only to give extra time per question; it is not a medical assessment and never leaves the device.
  • Reminders: local notifications scheduled by the device; no push service, no device identifiers.
  • Children: we collect no data from anyone, including children.
  • Deletion: Me → Delete my data erases everything immediately; uninstalling also removes it.
  • Changes: if optional sync is added later, this policy will be updated and your consent requested first.
